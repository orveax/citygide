## Six evidence upgrades

1. **NC-56 — FRA Financial District branch**: the FRA's October 2026 announcement confirms that Cabinet Decision 4049/2025 **established** a branch in the Financial District. Exact branch address, operational opening and public counter remain unconfirmed. The role is changed from `Public Service Point` to `Facility` and the name now expressly says the address remains under verification. [FRA first-party release](https://fra.gov.eg/fra_news/الأول-من-نوعه-خارج-العاصمة-افتتاح-مكتب/).
2. **NC-83 — Gates/AUDAZ**: Gates' own company history identifies the administrative twin buildings and Financial District. Developer asset, not developer HQ or individual tenant. [Gates first-party](https://gatesdevelopments.com/about-us/).
3. **NC-88 — Rwabet/SELA Business Hub**: the appointed architect independently confirms Rwabet's development, the financial/government district setting and `In Progress` status. [MOD+Partners](https://modpluspartners.com/projects/sela-business-hub/).
4. **NC-89 — Pyramids/Grand Square**: the developer's project page and inauguration report document the location at the ministerial entrance and the mall's 2022 opening, not any individual outlet. [Developer project](https://site.pyramidsdevelopments.com/grand-square/), [inauguration](https://pyramidsdevelopments.com/new/new.php?id=1).
5. **NC-90 — Allianz Egypt planned HQ**: the design and site-supervision consultant names the Allianz Headquarters project **Plot 11-B2, Financial District**. This validates a project, not a functioning branch. [Archimid Architects](https://archimid.com/portfolio/allianz-2/).
6. **NC-91 — El Batal/Rock Capital 1**: Rock Developments' own project portfolio unequivocally places it in **Governmental District Block #1**, so we corrected the old `Financial District / 26B.1` description. Existing building **NC-147** is also in Government District. [Rock Developments](https://rock-developments.com/portfolio/rock-capital1-new-capital/).
