## Rights and integrity gates

1. **Do not infer rights to architectural works from the photographer's CC/license statement alone.** [Wikimedia Commons' Egypt copyright guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Copyright_rules_by_territory/Egypt/en) treats Egypt as lacking dependable commercially reusable freedom-of-panorama coverage.
2. CC BY files require creator, license link and modification notice; CC BY-SA has additional share-alike conditions for adapted work. See [BY](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
3. [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license) allow broad free web uses subject to conditions; they do not prove rights held by architects, brands or third parties.
4. Complete a human visual QA of the full-resolution files (correct landmark, crop, light, artifact and exposure), then record credits, original URL, derivative dimensions, hash and alt text before a website image substitution.
5. No evidence of tenant reception or safe visitor entrance is inferred from any picture. Keep the existing [D37 protections](D37-actual-occupancy.md).
