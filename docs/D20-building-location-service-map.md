2. **Project developer ≠ office tenant**. “Gates — AUDAZ”, “New Plan — ELEVEN”, “JADEER — OBEX”, etc. are asset relationships, not confirmed occupied offices.
3. First-party EDGE explicitly lists C5, on-site reception, private offices, meeting rooms, access control and direct contact: https://edgeinnovationcenter.com/locations/new-administrative-capital . The exact public entrance remains unanswered.

6. Grand Square opening is explicitly reported by the developer but no individual tenants, current shops, or hours were imported without confirmation.
