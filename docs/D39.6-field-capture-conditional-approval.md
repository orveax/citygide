## Conditional approval: two independent decisions
**A. Listing eligibility** is whether a place may be described as an institution/address in the public guide, without implying open-to-public service.

**B. Route/visitor readiness** is whether this **particular service unit** has a visitor-accessible, proven arrival point, schedule and phone-level navigation. **B can never be inferred from A**.

| Level | What it permits | Minimum evidence | Reviewer/approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C0 — Research/HOLD | Internal candidate only | Source register with conflicts visible | Data reviewer |
| C1 — Address-only candidate | Editorially reviewable name/plot/address; **no public directions, no “open now,” no visitor-gate promise** | Primary institution identity + address + independent conflict triage; visitor status explicitly unknown | Data reviewer → Founder must approve individual publication |
| C2 — Confirmed public service (without door) | Service description and verified schedule may be editorially reviewed, **still no “navigate to entrance”** | Recent unit-specific public-service and hours evidence; missing entrance shown clearly | Data reviewer → Founder |
| C3 — Visitor entrance ready | Door/vehicle bay route and appropriate “Open in Google Maps” | All D39.4 gates G1–G8 including authorized geo-photo/annotated official plan, approach, service hours, desktop and real phone route test | Independent QA reviewer → Founder; deployer applies separately |

**Status rule:** C1 or C2 is a *candidate for approval*, not actual Published. Field evidence can fail and keep the unit at C0. Even C3 requires explicit release approval, not automatic promotion.

## Reusable on-site capture sheet — one per unit
Complete exactly one record per service, retaining `NULL` for unknowns. Do not photograph restricted facilities, guards, visitors, protected entry systems, or internal security details; obtain permission before photography.

| Field | Capture rule |
| --- | --- |
| `recordId` / `serviceUnit` | Stable Master ID and actual customer-service function |
| `collector` / `capturedAt` | Authorized field verifier; Egypt-local date/time |
| `visitorAllowed` / `conditions` | Yes/no/unknown, public vs staff/VIP, permit/booking/ID requirements |
| `entranceName` / `building` / `floor` | Visible sign or official site explanation; no guess |
| `entranceLatLng` / `gpsAccuracyM` | Reading from **permitted visitor entrance or vehicle bay**, GPS accuracy and capture method |
| `approachRoad` / `dropoff` | Accessible public road, driving end, pedestrian continuation, whether one-way or gated |
| `accessibility` | Ramp/steps/elevator **as observed**, distinct from official accessibility statements |
| `publicDays` / `publicHours` | Evidence of service reception; separate days and times; record exact display/source |
| `photos` / `permissions` | Source URLs/files only if capture allowed; photo context, rights, date; redact people |
| `desktopRouteTest` / `phoneRouteTest` | Arrival endpoint, OS/app, date/time, safe last metres, pass/fail |
| `conflictResolution` | Compare official institution link, Google listing and field pin; reasons for deviation |
| `reviewer` / `founderDecision` | Independent QA and human release decision, date and change log |

### Precision/freshness controls (proposed)
- Initial fixed entrance survey: aim for outdoor GPS accuracy **≤20m**. If worse, retain `entrance_verified=false` until supported by approved site plan, multiple readings and/or distinctive ground-level evidence. Accuracy alone never proves a door.
- Fixed bank locations: use recent inspection/official material at initial release; review at least every **180 days**, and whenever a credible correction is reported.
- Mobile MFA unit: demand confirmed same-week current stop before a location claim; where mobility is frequent, **same-day confirmation** for any “go now” promise. A March opening photograph cannot prove November position.
- Conflicts **>30m** between intended entrance and route endpoint trigger independent recheck; this is a QA alert, not an automatic GPS truth rule.

## Review and responsibility
1. **Field Verifier — Unassigned (responsible):** collects evidence lawfully; can submit but cannot self-approve.
2. **Independent Data/Map QA — Unassigned (responsible):** validates source, route endpoint, entrance precision and contradictions; reports PASS/FAIL/NEEDS-EVIDENCE.
3. **Founder / Mohamed Adel (accountable for release):** makes individual publishing and routing approval decisions after QA; no approval is presumed.
4. **Site Maintainer (implementation after approval):** applies only approved fields, tests responsive navigation and keeps rollback evidence.
5. **Reviewer log:** keep before/after coordinate, source URLs, who approved, approval time and reversal conditions.

## Stage gates and release decision
- Gate F1 — Field evidence captured legally and with source/date/precision.
- Gate F2 — Service/public accessibility and schedule verified at the specific unit.
- Gate F3 — First-party/third-party discrepancy resolved by documented evidence.
- Gate F4 — End-to-end driving and pedestrian/accessible route tested on a real phone and desktop.
- Gate F5 — Independent QA signs acceptance; Founder approves the exact change.
- Gate F6 — Public release QA/rollback plan reviewed. Only then may `visitorEntrance`, `routingState` and relevant public copy change.
