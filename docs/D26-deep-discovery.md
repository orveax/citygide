## Reconciliation safeguards

- Cross-checked every candidate against NC-1–201 for identity, branch and building collisions.
- Read adjacent rows of ITDA primary PDFs, rather than relying on search snippets. **Unit 505 CS06 belongs to Proptech for Artificial Intelligence, not the next entry Ready Translation; unit 305 CS26 belongs to Concelio Studio Consultants, not the next entry Sherif Conferences Center.** Both false-positive matches were excluded.
- Distinguished an authority **inside a ministry**, a separately identified **service point**, a **commercially registered company**, and an **announced building**.
- Ignored facilities clearly outside the two target districts, including the airport, residential zones, central business towers outside the financial boundary and the QNB City of Arts ATM.
- Notion was created and reconciled first; only then was `data/exploration.json` updated using the D26 register.
