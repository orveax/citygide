### Route semantics
- Maps renders directions to the selected coordinate or POI, **not necessarily to a pedestrian-friendly or accessible customer gate**.
- The visual dotted section, the vague “banks and finance district” destination, and routes via unnamed internal roads are **last-metre risk flags**. They are not proof of road closure, illegal access or a particular gate location.
- Google Maps travel distances and times are volatile. They are retained only as QA observations; do not expose as permanent directory data.
- [Google Maps URLs documentation](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started) prescribes a cross-platform directions link format `https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=LAT%2CLNG&travelmode=driving`. `destination_place_id` is an option when the correct place identity is verified. **This is a future implementation specification, not an approved service destination**.
- Actual smartphone browser/app acceptance and live turn-by-turn route to a verified entrance remain **NOT TESTED**.

## Field evidence collection — acceptance checklist (unexecuted)
The next onsite/authorized confirmation must be per **service unit**, not per plot. Log **collector, date/time, method, source rights and reviewer** with each item. Avoid restricted photography of government/security areas and respect site rules.

1. **Confirm service itself** — branch/service desk sign, public entry policy and whether customers are presently accepted. Separate regular and digital/SME facilities.
2. **Capture permitted entrance evidence** — public-facing signage and door label, accessible approach, wheelchair ramp location if applicable; take photos only where authorized. Do not include identifiable visitors or security-sensitive detail.
3. **Capture position with explicit precision** — geotag of permitted visitor door/vehicle bay, GPS accuracy estimate, fronting road, side of building, gate number, pedestrian entrance and drop-off position. Do not reuse building centroid.
4. **Check operating schedule** — dated public signage or official branch-specific hours, **days separately from times**; mobile units require day-of-visit stop validation.
5. **Independent reviewer** — compare against original official locator and third-party maps, resolve deviations, label false positives.
6. **Desktop + actual smartphone route test** — driving route end, safe pedestrian finish, opening navigation from City Guide, permitted accessible entrance; record pass/fail with evidence and time.
7. **Approval** — owner signs service-specific acceptance checklist before publishing `visitorEntrance` and `visitor_ready`.
