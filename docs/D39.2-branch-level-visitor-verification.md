## Critical correction: MFA special hours do NOT apply to the capital

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs June 2026 announcement:
- lists **six** extended-hours attestation offices (09:00–19:00 Sunday–Thursday and 10:00–13:00 Saturday);
- *separately* lists the New Capital office among the other offices under **ordinary Sunday–Thursday official working hours**, without numerical time.

Do not publish the extended-hours window for NC-15. A search snippet carrying both lists created a misleading summary; reading the full official document resolves it.

- https://www.mfa.gov.eg/ar/Ministers/Details/MinistryNews/5659
